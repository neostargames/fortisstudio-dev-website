Wordingo

Wordingo is a word puzzle game combining crossword puzzles with fun Jigsaw-style block placement. Players solve clues, arrange letter blocks, rotate pieces, and complete words to transform each puzzle from grey to colorful.

Easy to learn but increasingly challenging, Wordingo gives you a relaxing way to challenge your vocabulary, pattern recognition, and problem-solving skills.

  • 250+ Handcrafted Levels
  • Daily Word Challenges
  • Hints & Boosts
  • Offline Play
  • 6 Additional Minigames

Jelly Jam

Jelly Jam is a colorful match-3 puzzle game where players match adorable jelly characters, complete levels, and take on exciting challenges.

Easy to learn, satisfying to play, and packed with colorful puzzles.

  • Hundreds of Fun Levels
  • Colorful Graphics & Effects
  • Powerful Boosters
  • Offline Play
  • Challenging Gameplay
